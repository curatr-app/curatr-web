Privacy policy

Updated: October 18, 2026
This policy applies to the curatr app (iPhone and Android) and this website. It explains in plain language which data we process, why, and how you can use your rights.

1Who is responsible?

Paul Scheuble, as operator of curatr, is responsible for data processing (address see legal notice). For privacy questions write to curationapp@gmx.de.

2Which data does curatr process, and why?

curatr is a social curation app for restaurants, cafés and bars: you rate places, see your friends' recommendations and discover new places. For this we process:

DataPurposeLegal basis

3Where is the data stored, and who helps us?

We use technical service providers that process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in). The data is stored on servers in the EU.
  • Expo (sending notifications) as well as Apple and Google (delivering notifications on iPhone and Android). Data may be exchanged with servers outside the EU; the providers use the safeguards provided by the EU for this (for example standard contractual clauses).
  • MapTiler (map images). When the map loads, your IP address is sent to the map provider. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors.

We do not sell your data and do not pass it on for advertising. There is no advertising and no tracking by advertising or analytics services.

4What do other users see of you?

You decide what others see, in “Privacy” in your profile: Everyone, Followers only or Nobody (for bio, badges, top picks, recent visits, followers and following).

Name, username and profile picture are always visible to other users so the app works socially. Hints such as “friend was there” or “you both want to go” exist only between people who follow each other. You can turn this feature off in “Personalize”.

5How long do we keep data?

As long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all related data at any time in the app (Profile → Edit profile → Delete account) or request it by email, see delete account. Legal retention duties remain unaffected. Technical access data is kept by providers only briefly.

6Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can withdraw a given consent at any time with effect for the future. Just write to the address below. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
