Help & support

Updated: October 18, 2026

Contact

Write to us at curationapp@gmx.de. We usually reply within a few days. In the app you can also write directly under Profile → Give feedback.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time. Your profile and all related data are removed (visits, ratings, photos, lists, comments, follows).

In the app:

  1. Open Profile and tap Edit profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted and cannot be restored.

Without the app: Write to us from the email address of your account at curationapp@gmx.de with the subject “Delete account”. We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see my visits and top picks?
You decide under Profile → Privacy (everyone, followers only, nobody). Name, username and profile picture are always visible.

How do I report a place or content?
Use the menu (three dots) on places and visits and choose Report. We review every report.

A place is missing or wrong.
You can add missing places in the app. For changes use Suggest a change on the place page.

I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password when signing in. You will get a link by email.
